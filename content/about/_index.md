Hi, I’m *Yuanhe Song*, or you can just call me *Franz*. I currently live in Germany and write here about reading, films, language learning, everyday life, and the things that stay with me. I come from a background in physics and have also worked in data analysis and engineering.

## A Brief Timeline

**2025–present** · Hamburg, Germany \
Relocated to Hamburg and am now building a new life in Germany.

**2022–2025** · Shanghai, China \
Worked as a test and validation engineer at a company providing software solutions for semiconductor manufacturing and chip design. My work mainly involved software product validation and data-related automation.

**2016–2022** · Shanghai, China \
Worked in academic research in condensed matter physics, focusing on the experimental study of superconducting materials.

**2012–2016** · Shandong, China \
Completed my B.Sc. in Physics.
